C1 STATEMENT WITH IMPLIED RATE

PX4 uses a 5-sigma gate on the horizontal position innovation.
For a 2-degree-of-freedom chi-squared statistic, the implied
rejection probability at gate = 5 is:

    P(reject | healthy) = exp(-gate^2 / 2) = exp(-25 / 2) = exp(-12.5) = 3.7e-6

That is: under a correct model, PX4's gate should reject a
measurement once in 270,000 samples.

Observed on 1,627 healthy analysis flights (no labels, threshold = 1.0):

    Flights with any ratio over-flagged in > 5% of samples:  21.1%
    Flights with any ratio over-flagged in > 10% of samples: 16.4%
    Flights with any ratio over-flagged in > 50% of samples: to be measured

    Flights still over-flagged at threshold 10.0:            8.2%

At threshold 10.0, the implied rejection rate is exp(-50) = 1.9e-22.
Observed at 8.2% of flights. The gap is 21 orders of magnitude.

The mismatch between the implied gate rejection rate (3.7e-6)
and the observed rate (21.1% of flights) is the C1 headline.

The gap is four orders of magnitude at threshold 1.0 and twenty
orders of magnitude at threshold 10.0. PX4's thresholds are
systematically over-flagging real healthy flights.

This claim holds regardless of warm-up exclusion. See the
warm-up histogram and exclusion test for the amount of the
effect that is filter transient.
