anomaly detection on real UAV telemetry, conformal prediction for

coverage guarantees, firmware heterogeneity.

\- \*\*Quartile:\*\* Q2 (increasing to Q1)

\## Recommendation



\*\*Submit to PHM Society IJPHM first.\*\* Sir suggested it. It is open access.

Q2 with Q1 trajectory.

\## What we would need to add for a Q1 journal



\- Larger corpus (currently 1,627 flights; Q1 journals prefer 10,000+)

\- Comparison against published anomaly detectors (not just PX4 threshold)

\- Ablation on individual ratios

\- False alarm rate analysis at different operating points



\## What we already have that PHM cares about



\- Real flight data (not synthetic)

\- Physical fault labels (motor saturation)

\- Coverage guarantees (conformal)

\- Statistical rigor (confidence intervals, contamination control)

\- Reproducible pipeline (code + data available)
