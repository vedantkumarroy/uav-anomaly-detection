Q6: Are the 10^13 values in gps_hpos and gps_vpos sentinels or
    division-by-zero?

Answer: Neither. They are the squared normalized innovation used
by PX4's EKF2:

    innovation_test_ratio = innovation^2 / (gate_size^2 * variance)

Verified by reconstruction on log 37f5014b-c8fc-447d-9974-f72adcd6ff34:

  sample 12:
    innovation = -1.3492e+07
    variance   = 2.0928
    gate_size  = 5   (PX4 default EKF2_GPS_P_GATE)
    reconstructed = (1.3492e7)^2 / (25 * 2.0928) = 3.4792e+12
    logged ratio  = 3.4792e+12   (exact match)

  sample 13:
    innovation = -1.3492e+07
    variance   = 2.1094
    reconstructed = (1.3492e7)^2 / (25 * 2.1094) = 3.4519e+12
    logged ratio  = 3.4519e+12   (exact match)

  sample 14:
    innovation = -1.3492e+07
    variance   = 2.1845
    reconstructed = (1.3492e7)^2 / (25 * 2.1845) = 3.3332e+12
    logged ratio  = 3.3332e+12   (exact match)

The relationship is verified. The gate size is 5, matching the PX4
default.

What this means:

  1. The ratio is a squared quantity. It has a nonlinear dependence
     on the innovation variance. A small variance (typical) with a
     moderately large innovation gives a ratio in the 10^6 to 10^12
     range.

  2. When this ratio exceeds 1.0, PX4 rejects the measurement. At
     10^12 the rejection is complete. The flight is still classified
     as healthy by every upstream quality gate.

  3. This strengthens the C1 result. On 21 percent of healthy flights,
     PX4's own threshold is exceeded in more than 5 percent of samples.
     The ratio exceeds by many orders of magnitude, not marginally.

  4. The log(1+x) and 99.9th percentile clip are correct stability
     fixes. But the deeper insight is that the ratio is heavy-tailed
     by construction. Papers analyzing it should note this.

  5. No sentinel values were found. No division by exactly zero
     (variance is always >= 0.3 in the sampled logs). The 10^12 values
     are the real arithmetic PX4 performs.
